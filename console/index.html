      A run of the model, not of physics. The droplet shrinks along the d-squared law at the burning rate the model predicts. Whether this run burns all its fuel is drawn at random with the model's odds, and when it goes out, the size it stops at is borrowed from a real FLEX test close to these settings. The flame, igniter wires, soot shell and the faint haze after a heptane flame goes out (FLEX found that a cool flame can carry on there) are drawn to look like the experiment. They are illustration, not footage and not modelled.
