## 8. What does DAFT have to do with smoke detection?

Asked from the research page (looking at PSI-47, list ranked for Detection).

> DAFT / DAFT-2 [PSI-47] was designed to mitigate risks for the Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment (SAME). It tested whether a modified commercial particulate counter, the TSI P-Trak, would function accurately in microgravity. Because the original P-Trak relied on gravity to recycle isopropyl alcohol, it was modified to use capillary forces instead. This research helps develop more reliable and sensitive fire and smoke detection systems for future spacecraft.

## What these answers cannot tell you

- It answers in at most about 110 words, so lists are often incomplete. Treat a missing investigation as "not mentioned", not as "not relevant".
